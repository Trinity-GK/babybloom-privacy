Privacy Policy for Baby Bloom

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android apps Baby Bloom (com.renchan.babybloom.free) and Baby Bloom Full (com.renchan.babybloom.full). Baby Bloom is a musical play app for babies and young children.

Information the app does not collect

Baby Bloom does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no in-app purchases. It does not use third-party software kits that collect data. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to any other party.

Information stored on the device

The app saves a few choices in its private storage on the device so they are still there the next time it opens. Those choices are the language, whether sound is on, autoplay, the volume limit, the frame rate, how long the parent lock must be held, and the last activity, scene, and song. This information stays on the device. It is not backed up by the app and it is not uploaded.

Microphone

Baby Bloom Full can use the microphone only when a parent records a short sound to use as an instrument. The recording stays in the app’s private storage on the device and is used only to play notes inside the app. It is never uploaded, shared, or used to identify anyone. Recording again replaces the previous sound. The free edition does not request microphone access.

Children

The app is made for babies and young children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parent settings are behind a lock that must be held. Children are not asked to enter personal information.

Google Play

If you install Baby Bloom from Google Play, Google may process information about the download under Google’s Privacy Policy. Baby Bloom does not receive that information, and it does not share any information with Google.

How to delete information

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the device settings, deletes the saved choices and any recording. There is no account to delete and no copy of this information on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will show a new date at the top. Continued use of the app after the page is updated means the new policy applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Google Play listing for Baby Bloom.
